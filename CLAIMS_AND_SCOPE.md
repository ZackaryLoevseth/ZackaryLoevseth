# Claims and scope

The profile summarizes public research artifacts and their stated status. It
does not claim peer review, absolute novelty, record priority, a solution of an
open global problem, or comparative causal superiority of AI-assisted methods.

The public Erdős Problem #409 certificate proves only
`F(400000287233629)=104`, with the consequence `sup_n F(n) >= 104`. It does not
claim world-record priority, unboundedness, global maximality, basin infinitude,
density, inverse-tree completeness, or a solution of the global problem.

The three featured repositories are public GitHub preprint releases. They are
not peer-reviewed or accepted publications and have not been submitted to a
journal or arXiv. Personal, legal, authorship, affiliation, funding/conflict,
licensing, and final-submission attestations remain human-only.
